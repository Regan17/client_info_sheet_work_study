--- PUNJABI SCRIPT (romanized; speak naturally) ---

Eh section “Other Country Residency” baare hai.

Pehla sawaal eh hai: kya tusi India te Canada to alawa kise hor desh vich chhe mahine ya us ton vadd reh chuke ho?

Jé tusi sirf India vich reh chuke ho, ya sirf Canada vich, ya dono jagah rehna chhota-chhota si — matlab kise hor desh vich lagatar chhe mahine ton vadd nahi — ta “No” chuno.

Jé tusi kise hor desh vich — jiven USA, UK, Australia, UAE, ya koi hor — chhe mahine ya us ton vadd reh chuke ho, ta “Yes” chuno.

Jé “Yes” hai, ta thalle di jaankari likhni hai:

“From” — rehaish kab shuru hoi: mahina te saal, jiven 01/2020.

“To” — rehaish kab khatam hoi: mahina te saal, jiven 06/2022.

“Country Name” — desh da poora naam.

“Status” — oh dio ki tusi keho bande ho: jiven Work Visa, Study Visa, Visitor, PR, ya Permanent Resident.

“Purpose of Stay” — thodi jahi jaankari ke kyon reh rahe si: jiven study, job, business, ya family.

Yaad rakho: India apna desh hai te Canada alag — in dono nu is section vich likhna nahi. Sirf hor deshaan di rehaish dasni hai.

--- ENGLISH REFERENCE (for staff / recorder) ---

This section asks whether the client lived in any country other than India (home country) and Canada for six months or more in total.

If they only lived in India and/or Canada, or any stay elsewhere was under six months, select No.

If they lived elsewhere (e.g. USA, UK, Australia, UAE) for six months or more, select Yes and complete From, To, Country Name, Status, and Purpose of Stay (MM/YYYY for dates).

Do not list India or Canada here — only other countries.
